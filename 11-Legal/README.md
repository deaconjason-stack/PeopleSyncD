# 11 — Legal

**Domain ID:** PSD-DOM-LEGAL-011  
**Accountable function:** Executive and Legal Counsel  
**Purpose:** Protect intellectual property, govern licensing and contracts, maintain corporate and trademark records, and align product behavior with legal obligations.

## Canonical sources

- `LICENSE`
- `docs/licensing/`
- `CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md`
- `SECURITY.md`
- Approved legal records, policies, templates, and contract indexes maintained under controlled access

## Required artifacts

- Intellectual-property ownership, assignment, invention, and open-source records
- Product, source, commercial, OEM, partner, and customer licensing terms
- Trademark, domain, copyright, patent, and brand-usage records
- Customer, partner, vendor, employment, contractor, confidentiality, and data-processing agreements
- Privacy, acceptable-use, support, security, retention, AI, and marketplace policies
- Contract obligation register linked to product, security, operations, and customer commitments
- Legal review and approval records for material claims and releases

## Rules

- The repository license is proprietary unless an approved file explicitly states otherwise.
- Third-party dependencies require license review and accurate SBOM representation.
- Contracts cannot promise unsupported functionality, certification, service levels, or deployment profiles.
- Personal, customer, legal, and privileged documents require controlled access and retention.
- AI-generated legal content is not legal approval and must be reviewed by authorized counsel where required.
- Trademarks and company names must use approved forms and ownership records.

## Completion gate

A commercial release or customer commitment is legally ready only when applicable licensing, IP, privacy, contract, policy, claim, third-party, and jurisdictional reviews are complete and obligations are traceable to owners and controls.
