# Domonique 2.0 AI Platform Test Strategy

## Required suites

- Tenant and identity isolation
- Permission and assistant-mode narrowing
- Grounding and citation accuracy
- Uncertainty and refusal
- Direct and indirect prompt injection
- Malicious document and source poisoning
- Tool allowlisting and schema validation
- Approval, stale state, idempotency, and rollback
- Memory review, correction, deletion, retention, and legal hold
- Voice privacy and visual confirmation
- Model-provider outage and fallback
- Kill-switch operation
- Bias, accessibility, latency, throughput, and cost budgets

Every executable case receives a `PSD-TEST-AI-*` identifier before certification.
