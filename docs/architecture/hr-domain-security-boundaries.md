# HR Domain Security Boundaries

## Ordinary workforce data

Person summaries, employment status, assignment, onboarding status, credentials, and training are exposed only according to role, relationship, classification, and tenant scope.

## Highly confidential data

Identity documents, tax records, medical or accommodation information, screening reports, compensation, restricted cases, investigations, and highly confidential documents require separate permissions and may require fresh authentication.

## Governance data

Board appointments, votes, resolutions, and governance authority remain owned by the Board service.

## AI boundary

Domonique 2.0 uses narrow authorized tools, cannot access unrestricted databases, and cannot make high-impact employment, disciplinary, legal, accommodation, compensation, or governance decisions.
