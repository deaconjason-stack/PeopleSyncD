# Domonique 2.0 AI Platform

Domonique 2.0 is the permission-aware intelligence layer.

## Components

- AI orchestration
- Knowledge engine
- Policy engine
- Tool engine
- Approval engine
- Memory engine
- Audit engine

## Modes

Founder Assistant, HR Assistant, Governance Assistant, Board Assistant, and Compliance Assistant.

## Non-negotiable rule

AI recommends and prepares. Authorized humans approve high-impact actions. Domonique 2.0 cannot bypass tenant isolation, permissions, source grounding, or audit requirements.
