# HR Domain Context

PeopleSyncD HR is the first business application on the shared enterprise platform.

```text
Identity + Organization + Permissions
                ↓
Person → Employment/Worker → Onboarding
                ↓              ↓
Credentials/Training      Documents/Workflow
                ↓              ↓
HR Cases ← Audit/Notifications/AI
                ↓
Founder · HR · Manager · Employee Experiences
```

Board authority remains a separate governance context. HR coordinates approved onboarding and directory references without owning appointments, votes, resolutions, or board membership changes.
