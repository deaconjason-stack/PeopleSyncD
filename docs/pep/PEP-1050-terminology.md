# PEP-1050: Terminology

- Status: Accepted

Person: master human identity record. Worker: a person's service relationship with an organization. Organization: tenant or governed operating entity. Board appointment: time-bound governance authority. Document: versioned governed file or record. Workflow: stateful business process. Audit event: append-only evidence. Domonique 2.0: permission-aware AI orchestration layer, not an independent decision-maker.
