# Customer Administrator Manual

Customer administrators manage authorized tenant setup, identity federation, roles, memberships, licensing, configuration, branding, integrations, document retention, notifications, workflow templates, AI availability, audit access, exports, backups, upgrades, and support requests.

Administrators cannot bypass platform security minima, alter immutable audit evidence, access another tenant, expose secrets, or grant themselves permissions outside contractual and organizational authority.

Every privileged change is auditable and should follow separation of duties where required.
