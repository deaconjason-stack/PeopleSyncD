# Domonique 2.0 Assistant Modes and Founder Brief Specification

## Modes

Founder, Employee, Manager, HR, Board, Compliance, and Administrator modes. Mode selection narrows available data and tools; it never expands the caller's permissions.

## Founder Brief

The Founder Brief summarizes authorized company status, pending approvals, workforce risks, onboarding, credentials, documents, security events, governance activity, and next actions. Every material statement links to source evidence or is labeled as an inference.

## Governance safeguard

The active MediSyncD Technologies board is Jason Henderson, Domonique Danielle Henderson, and Marietta Jessup only. Board summaries obtain active membership from the Board service.
