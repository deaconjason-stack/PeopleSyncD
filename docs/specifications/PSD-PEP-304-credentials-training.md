# Credentials and Training Specification

## Purpose

Track role-required licenses, certifications, training assignments, completions, expirations, verification evidence, and renewal workflows.

## Rules

- Credential types and requirements are organization-configurable.
- Verification source, reviewer, date, expiration, and document evidence are recorded.
- Expiration alerts respect permissions and privacy.
- A credential record does not independently establish legal eligibility; authorized review remains responsible.
- Training completions preserve course version and evidence.
