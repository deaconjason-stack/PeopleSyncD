# Onboarding Service Specification

## Purpose

Coordinate repeatable, role-aware onboarding through governed templates, tasks, documents, training, equipment, approvals, deadlines, exceptions, and readiness evidence.

## Initial templates

- General Employee
- Software Developer
- Academy Instructor
- Board Member coordination

## Rules

- Templates are versioned and organization-scoped.
- Critical tasks cannot be waived without authorized approval.
- Readiness scores identify completion status but do not replace human hiring authority.
- Workers see only their assigned tasks and permitted documents.
- Every task, exception, reminder, approval, and completion is auditable.
- Board Member onboarding does not create the appointment; it coordinates approved governance onboarding.
