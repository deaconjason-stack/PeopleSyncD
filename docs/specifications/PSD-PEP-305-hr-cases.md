# HR Cases and Concerns Specification

## Purpose

Provide restricted workflows for employee concerns, investigations, requests, corrective actions, accommodations coordination, and other sensitive HR matters.

## Security boundary

HR cases use separate permissions, restricted search, minimized notifications, explicit assignment, fresh authentication for highly confidential access, and enhanced audit evidence.

## AI rule

Domonique 2.0 may organize facts, summarize authorized records, draft neutral communications, and identify missing information. It cannot determine guilt, credibility, discipline, termination, legal conclusions, accommodation outcomes, or retaliation risk.
