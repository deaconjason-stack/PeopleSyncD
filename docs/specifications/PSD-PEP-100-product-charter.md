# PeopleSyncD Enterprise Platform Product Charter

## Product definition

PeopleSyncD Enterprise Platform is an AI-powered enterprise workforce operating system. PeopleSyncD HR is the first application; the reusable platform is the long-term product.

## Product outcomes

The platform provides shared capabilities for identity, organizations, permissions, licensing, workflow, notifications, documents, audit, search, reporting, configuration, AI orchestration, integrations, telemetry, localization, and accessibility.

Business applications include HR, Recruiting, Payroll Integration, Learning, Governance, Compliance, Board Management, Projects, Assets, CRM, Help Desk, and Analytics.

## Deployment targets

- Cloud SaaS
- Private cloud
- On-premises
- Hybrid enterprise
- Government restricted environments

These are target deployment models and do not imply current certification.

## Engineering principles

- Domain-Driven Design
- Contract-First Development
- Security-First Engineering
- Documentation-Driven Development
- Test-Driven Verification
- Multi-tenant isolation
- Human approval for high-impact AI actions
- Evidence before claims

## Domonique 2.0

Domonique 2.0 is the governed AI intelligence layer. It uses approved tools, source-grounded retrieval, policy enforcement, approval workflows, memory controls, and complete AI audit evidence.

AI recommends and prepares. Authorized humans approve high-impact actions.

## Commercial targets

The product is intended to become white-label ready, OEM ready, government ready, healthcare ready, store ready, cloud ready, on-premises ready, hybrid ready, AI native, multi-tenant, and license managed.

Each readiness claim requires its own approved requirements and certification evidence.
