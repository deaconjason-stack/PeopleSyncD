# Employee Self-Service UI Specification

## Purpose

Allow workers to review permitted personal information, onboarding tasks, credentials, training, documents, notifications, and requests.

## Capabilities

View and propose corrections, complete assigned tasks, upload permitted evidence, acknowledge documents, request support, and ask Domonique 2.0 authorized questions.

## Boundaries

Employees cannot access another worker's information through search, direct URLs, exports, notifications, or AI prompts.
