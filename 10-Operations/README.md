# 10 — Operations

**Domain ID:** PSD-DOM-OPS-010  
**Accountable function:** Executive Operations, People Operations, Finance Operations, and Internal Systems  
**Purpose:** Run the company and platform through measurable objectives, departmental procedures, staffing, performance, risk, and internal controls.

## Canonical sources

- `docs/governance/program-offices.md`
- `docs/governance/`
- `docs/operations/`
- `infrastructure/monitoring/`
- Approved OKRs, KPIs, department manuals, hiring plans, and operating reviews maintained under this domain

## Required artifacts

- Company, product, engineering, security, customer, and financial OKRs
- KPI definitions, data sources, owners, targets, and review cadence
- Department charters, manuals, controls, escalation paths, and continuity plans
- Hiring plans, role descriptions, onboarding, access provisioning, and offboarding
- Performance expectations, review, coaching, succession, and accountability processes
- Internal systems inventory, owners, data classifications, and access reviews
- Budget, procurement, vendor, asset, and business-continuity controls
- Executive, board, operational, risk, and release review calendars

## Rules

- Every operational metric requires a definition, owner, source, target, and decision use.
- One person may hold multiple roles temporarily, but responsibilities and approval authority remain explicit.
- Access follows least privilege and is reviewed when roles change.
- Hiring or vendor commitments must map to approved budgets and accountable outcomes.
- Department manuals must match actual supported systems and controls.
- Operational exceptions require owners, deadlines, and escalation paths.

## Completion gate

An operating function is controlled only when ownership, procedures, metrics, systems, access, risks, escalation, continuity, and review evidence are documented and active.
