# 12 — Research

**Domain ID:** PSD-DOM-RES-012  
**Accountable function:** Research, Architecture, and Innovation  
**Purpose:** Explore future technologies and product opportunities without confusing experiments with approved platform capabilities.

## Canonical sources

- `ROADMAP.md`
- `docs/rfc/`
- Planned and experimental specifications under `docs/specifications/`
- Approved research records, prototypes, evaluations, and long-term roadmaps maintained under this domain

## Required artifacts

- Research questions, hypotheses, owners, methods, and success criteria
- Prior-art, competitive, technical, market, legal, and ethical reviews
- Prototype boundaries and synthetic-data requirements
- AI research, model, agent, knowledge, and evaluation studies
- Future infrastructure, interface, device, edge, privacy, and security investigations
- Experiment results, limitations, reproducibility, and recommendations
- Technology adoption, deferment, retirement, and transfer decisions
- Long-term roadmaps separated from committed release plans

## Rules

- Research artifacts are not production specifications or customer commitments.
- Experiments use isolated environments and synthetic or explicitly authorized data.
- High-risk AI, identity, security, privacy, employment, legal, or safety research requires prior review.
- Results must record negative findings and limitations, not only promising outcomes.
- Transfer into Product or Engineering requires an approved RFC, requirements, architecture review, and implementation plan.
- Patent, publication, open-source, and disclosure decisions require legal review.

## Completion gate

A research outcome may enter the product roadmap only when evidence, risks, ownership, legal and ethical review, architecture impact, operational cost, and measurable customer value have been evaluated and approved.
