# 01 — Business

**Domain ID:** PSD-DOM-BUS-001  
**Accountable function:** Executive, Finance, Commercial, and Partnerships  
**Purpose:** Connect company strategy, market demand, revenue, capital, partnerships, and measurable outcomes to product investment.

## Canonical sources

- `ROADMAP.md`
- `docs/licensing/`
- `docs/releases/`
- `docs/governance/program-offices.md`
- Approved business goals, financial models, pricing decisions, and board records stored under this domain

## Required artifacts

- Business model and value proposition
- Market segmentation and competitive strategy
- Packaging, pricing, discount, and entitlement policy
- Sales stages, qualification, forecasting, and contracting model
- Marketing positioning, evidence standards, and brand controls
- Customer-success economics and retention model
- Partnership and channel strategy
- Investor relations, capital plan, and approved financial models
- Business goals with owners, metrics, targets, and review cadence

## Interfaces

Business strategy receives market evidence, customer feedback, product cost, operational capacity, legal constraints, and board direction. It emits governed business goals, investment priorities, pricing, commercial requirements, and success metrics.

## Rules

- Revenue claims, market claims, savings claims, and return-on-investment claims require evidence and stated assumptions.
- Pricing must map to licensing and entitlement behavior.
- Customer commitments must be traceable to supported product capabilities and release plans.
- Financial models must distinguish actuals, approved forecasts, scenarios, and aspirations.
- Investor materials must use the same factual release and certification boundaries as engineering documentation.

## Completion gate

A business initiative may enter product planning only when its accountable owner, target customer, measurable outcome, constraints, funding assumption, risk, and product impact are documented and approved.
