# HR Cases Requirements

- `PSD-REQ-HR-CASE-000001` Restrict case access through explicit assignment and permission.
- `PSD-REQ-HR-CASE-000002` Minimize case information in notifications and search.
- `PSD-REQ-HR-CASE-000003` Preserve immutable case timeline and evidence references.
- `PSD-REQ-HR-CASE-000004` Require fresh authentication for highly confidential access.
- `PSD-REQ-HR-CASE-000005` Prevent AI from making disciplinary or legal determinations.
