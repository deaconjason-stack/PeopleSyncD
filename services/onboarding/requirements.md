# Onboarding Requirements

- `PSD-REQ-HR-ONBOARD-000001` Launch onboarding from a versioned template.
- `PSD-REQ-HR-ONBOARD-000002` Assign role-aware tasks to authorized actors.
- `PSD-REQ-HR-ONBOARD-000003` Require approval to waive critical tasks.
- `PSD-REQ-HR-ONBOARD-000004` Calculate transparent readiness status.
- `PSD-REQ-HR-ONBOARD-000005` Preserve task, exception, approval, and completion evidence.
