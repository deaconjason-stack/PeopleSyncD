# Person Service Requirements

- `PSD-REQ-HR-PERSON-000001` Create a tenant-scoped Person record with classified contact information.
- `PSD-REQ-HR-PERSON-000002` Prevent unrestricted exposure of sensitive identity attributes.
- `PSD-REQ-HR-PERSON-000003` Preserve historical name and contact changes.
- `PSD-REQ-HR-PERSON-000004` Require reviewed, auditable merges for suspected duplicate records.
- `PSD-REQ-HR-PERSON-000005` Reject cross-tenant references.
