# Domonique 2.0 AI Service Requirements

- `PSD-REQ-AI-CONV-000001` Tenant-scoped permission-aware conversations
- `PSD-REQ-AI-GROUND-000001` Source-grounded answers with provenance
- `PSD-REQ-AI-TOOL-000001` Versioned permission-aware tools
- `PSD-REQ-AI-APPROVAL-000001` Human approval for high-impact actions
- `PSD-REQ-AI-MEMORY-000001` Tenant-isolated governed memory
- `PSD-REQ-AI-FOUNDER-000001` Source-linked Founder Brief
- `PSD-REQ-AI-VOICE-000001` Privacy-preserving voice activation
- `PSD-REQ-AI-SEC-000001` Prompt-injection and tool-abuse defense

All AI features must preserve tenant isolation, normal service authorization, audit evidence, and non-AI fallback behavior.
