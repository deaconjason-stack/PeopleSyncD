# Domonique 2.0 AI Deployment

Deploy the orchestration service separately from model providers and business databases. Use managed secrets, outbound allowlists, encryption, provider-specific data controls, health checks, circuit breakers, quotas, cost limits, audit integration, safety monitoring, and a tested kill switch.

A tenant may select approved cloud, private-cloud, on-premises, or hybrid model routes when supported by its edition and deployment agreement.
